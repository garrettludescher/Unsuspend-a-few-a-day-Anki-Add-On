<b>Daily Unsuspend</b> — release suspended cards a few at a time, automatically.

Have 400 suspended cards under a tag but only want to add 5 a day? Make a rule, pick the tags, set the number per day, and Daily Unsuspend unsuspends that many each day the first time you open Anki.

<b>Features</b>
• One rule per tag group. A rule can cover several tags, and picking a tag includes all of its sub-tags
• Set cards per day for each rule, and turn rules on or off
• Card order for each rule: order added, newest first, due position, random cards, or random notes (keeps siblings together)
• Shows how many cards are left and the date each rule will finish
• Runs once a day. Missed days are skipped by default, or turn on "Catch up missed days"
• A small pop-up shows what was unsuspended, with "View in Browser" and "Undo last batch"
• "Run now" button for an extra batch whenever you want one
• Tag picker built for big collections (tested with 50,000+ tags): a collapsible tree plus fast search
• Open it from Tools → Daily Unsuspend, the button on the deck screen, or the Browser's menu bar
• Glass-style interface that follows light and dark mode (can be turned off)

<b>Days off</b>
• Pick weekdays with no new cards, like Sundays
• Block specific dates in a calendar: click several days, drag across a range, or shift-click
• Overlapping blocks merge into the larger one (block 11/13, then 11/10–11/15, and you get one 11/10–11/15 block)
• Days off never count as missed days for catch-up, and the finish date skips them
• "Run now" still works on a day off if you want cards anyway

<b>Sync-safe</b>
• Settings live in your collection, so they sync across computers
• If a sync from another device wipes the add-on's settings, your rules and days off are restored automatically from a local backup

<b>Notes</b>
• Unsuspending happens in desktop Anki. Sync afterwards and the cards show up on AnkiMobile / AnkiDroid
• A rule unsuspends every suspended card with its tags, including leeches. Keep cards you want to stay suspended out of your rule's tags
• Tested on Anki 25.09.2
